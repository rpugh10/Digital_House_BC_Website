A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Getting your business online starts with a clear conversation. Here are a few things to know before we talk.

Have another question? Get in touch
How much will my website cost?

Starter is $550, Business is $800, and Business Plus is $1,200. Compare the included pages and features above, then use "Discuss your project" to tell us what you need.

How do I choose the right plan?

Start with the information your customers need and what you want your website to do. Tell us about your business, the pages you have in mind, and any features you need so we can discuss a suitable scope.

Can you help if my business has no website yet?

Yes. We build websites for local businesses in Kamloops, including businesses taking their first step online. You can start by sharing what you do and how customers currently find you.

What should I prepare before getting in touch?

A short description of your business, your services, and the information you want customers to find is a useful start. You can also share any existing logo, photos, or website examples you like.

How long does a website project take?

Timing depends on the agreed scope and the content needed for your website. We will discuss a timeline for your project rather than promise a fixed turnaround before understanding your needs.

What happens once my website is built?

Once your website is built, we'll review everything with you, make any remaining included adjustments, and prepare it for launch. We'll also explain how to get in touch if you need further changes or assistance in the future.
