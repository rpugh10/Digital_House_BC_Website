# Digital House BC Website

## Overview

### At Digital House, we believe every local business deserves a place online that feels like home.

### We're a web design and development company based in British Columbia, founded by two aspiring entrepreneurs and Thompson Rivers University students studying Computer Science and Tourism Management. Bringing together our interests in technology, business, and customer experience, we're focused on helping local businesses build a stronger online presence through modern, well-designed websites.

### Whether you're starting from scratch or looking to give your existing website a fresh look, we work closely with business owners to create something that reflects their business, connects with their customers, and gives them a website they're proud to share.
